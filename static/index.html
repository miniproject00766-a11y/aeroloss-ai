How AeroLoss AI computes financial loss: Visual drone inspection patches are processed through YOLOv8 for defect classification and area proxy extraction. The extracted spanwise location (r/R) applies Sandia National Labs & IEA Wind Task 46 airfoil polar degradation on the FFA-W3-241 airfoil ($Re = 10^7$). Local lift/drag changes ($\Delta C_l, \Delta C_d$) compute turbine-level daily lost generation ($\text{kWh/day}$) and revenue impact ($\text{₹/day}$) for payback evaluation.
