## 1. Executive Summary

This comprehensive dataset audit and readiness assessment establishes the empirical foundation for rebuilding **AeroLoss AI** from scratch in an evidence-based, mathematically sound manner. Prior to this assessment, claims existed in previous project documentation regarding high model accuracy ($m\text{AP}_{50} \approx 69\%$, $R^2 \approx 0.999$ for aerodynamic and energy loss regressors).

Our systematic, programmatic inspection across all original raw images, Pascal VOC XML annotations, external Kaggle/DTU datasets, SCADA logs, airfoil polars, processed patches, and existing model checkpoints reveals the following critical findings:

1. **Primary Defect Benchmark Integrity:**  
   The primary dataset in `data/raw/` comprises exactly **1,065 RGB inspection photographs** and **1,065 matching Pascal VOC XML files** containing **1,584 annotated defect objects** across 6 classes (`corrosion`, `crack`, `craze`, `hide_craze`, `surface_injure`, `thunderstrike`). All 1,065 XML files parse with zero syntax errors, zero negative/zero-area bounding boxes, zero out-of-boundary coordinate overflows, and 100% agreement with actual image pixel dimensions (1,064 images at $1024 \times 1024$ and 1 image at $788 \times 788$).

2. **Discovery of Cross-Split Data Leakage:**  
   Two exact duplicate image pairs exist in the primary benchmark:
   - `547.jpg` $\equiv$ `640.jpg`
   - `565.jpg` $\equiv$ `668.jpg`  
   In the existing YOLO partition (`data/yolo_dataset/`), `640.jpg` and `565.jpg` were allocated to the **train** split, while their identical counterparts `547.jpg` and `668.jpg` were allocated to the **val** split. Furthermore, near-duplicate drone photographs of the same blade defect captured from shifted viewpoints (perceptual dHash distance $\le 2$) were split across train and validation (e.g. `189.jpg` in train vs `172.jpg` in val). This confirms evaluation leakage in previous benchmark metrics.

3. **100% Patch Traceability Established:**  
   All **1,584 defect patches** in `data/processed/patches/` are 100% traceable to their originating raw images and exact XML coordinates via `data/processed/defect_features.csv`.

4. **The Synthetic Ground-Truth Tautology:**  
   The reported physics and energy loss regressors ($R^2 = 0.999$) were fitted entirely on `data/synthetic/aeroloss_multimodal_dataset.csv`. That dataset was generated by passing randomized numbers through the analytical `AeroLossPhysicsEngine` formula. The model merely learned an arithmetic formula. **Zero real-world ground-truth pairs connecting blade inspection photographs to actual SCADA power losses exist in the repository.**

5. **Turbine Rating Incompatibility:**  
   The SCADA dataset `data/raw/T1.csv` is from a commercial **~3.6 MW turbine** (peak active power $3,618.7\text{ kW}$ across 50,530 10-minute records). In contrast, the aerodynamic documentation (`IEA_Wind_Task_46_WP3.1_AEP_Loss-1.pdf`) and polar table (`FFA_W3_241_polar.csv`) are based on the **IEA 15-MW Offshore Reference Turbine**. Applying 15-MW equations directly to 3.6-MW operational data without geometric scaling induces severe scaling errors.

## 10. Missing Data and Unresolved Questions

1. **Absence of Empirical Defect-to-SCADA Ground Truth:**  
   The primary benchmark images have no timestamp metadata, turbine identifiers, or farm locations linkable to `T1.csv`. Real-world aerodynamic degradation must be computed using physically grounded BEM principles rather than end-to-end black-box regression.
2. **True Physical Dimension Scale ($mm/\text{pixel}$):**  
   The photographs lack an in-frame calibration target (e.g. photogrammetric fiducial marker or laser scale). Blade defect area can only be estimated as a percentage of blade chord or bounding box area proxy.
3. **Out-of-Domain Guardrail Calibration Set:**  
   The 30 images in `data/negative_evaluation_samples/` are insufficient to reliably calibrate Model 1 false positive rates.

## 11. Synthetic Data Policy & Recommendations

> [!CAUTION]
> **Synthetic Data Must NOT Be Used in Model Implementation at this Stage:**
> 1. Generating synthetic tabular rows or running heuristic physics simulators cannot substitute for real turbine measurements and creates false confidence (e.g. artificial $R^2 > 0.99$).
> 2. For computer vision defect detection, the minority classes (`thunderstrike` and `crack`) should be augmented using **empirically grounded image techniques** (targeted copy-paste augmentation from real patches, CLAHE contrast tuning, and horizontal/vertical flips) rather than generative synthetic models (GANs/diffusion) that risk producing unphysical aerodynamic artifacts.
